## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are **small text files stored on your device** (computer, mobile, tablet) when you visit a website.

They help websites:

- Function properly
- Improve performance
- Remember user preferences
- Enhance security
- Provide analytics insights

Cookies do not typically contain **personally identifiable information**, but they may be linked to personal data stored elsewhere.
