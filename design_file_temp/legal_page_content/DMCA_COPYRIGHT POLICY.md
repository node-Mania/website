## 1. Introduction

NodeMania respects intellectual property rights and expects all customers to do the same.

This **DMCA / Copyright Policy** outlines the procedure for reporting alleged copyright infringement on websites, servers, or infrastructure hosted by NodeMania.

We comply with applicable copyright laws, including the **U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA)** and international intellectual property regulations.

---

## 2. Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe that content hosted on NodeMania’s network infringes your copyright, you must submit a **written notice** containing the following information:

### Required Information

- Your full legal name and contact information (email, phone, address)
- Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed
- The exact **URL(s)** or **IP address** where the infringing material is located
- A statement that you have a **good faith belief** the use is unauthorized
- A statement that the **information provided is accurate**
- A statement made **under penalty of perjury** that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf
- Your **physical or electronic signature**

> Incomplete notices may not be processed.

---

## 3. DMCA Notice Submission

All copyright complaints must be submitted to:

**Email:** abuse@nodemania.com  
**Subject Line:** DMCA Takedown Notice

NodeMania may request **additional documentation** if necessary.

---

## 4. Counter-Notification Procedure

If you believe that material removed or disabled as a result of a DMCA notice was removed in **error or misidentification**, you may submit a **counter-notification** including:

- Your name, address, phone number, and email
- Identification of the material removed and its previous location
- A statement under penalty of perjury that you believe removal was a **mistake**
- Consent to the **jurisdiction of applicable courts**
- Your **physical or electronic signature**

Counter-notices must be sent to:

**abuse@nodemania.com**
