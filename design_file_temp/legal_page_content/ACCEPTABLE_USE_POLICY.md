# ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY (AUP)

**Effective Date:** January 30, 2026 
**Company:** NodeMania  
**Website:** https://www.nodemania.com

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (**“AUP”**) governs the use of all services provided by **NodeMania**, including but not limited to:

- Shared Web Hosting
- Business Hosting
- WordPress Hosting
- Cloud VPS
- Managed Cloud Servers
- Domain Services
- Email Services
- Monitoring Services
- Backup Solutions
- CDN Services
- Security Tools

This policy is designed to protect the **integrity, security, reliability, and performance** of our infrastructure and all customers using it.

By using NodeMania services, you agree to comply with this AUP.

---

## 2. General Prohibited Activities

You may **NOT** use NodeMania services to engage in, promote, or facilitate:

- Any illegal activity under applicable local, national, or international law
- Fraudulent or deceptive practices
- Identity theft or impersonation
- Financial scams or phishing schemes
- Hosting content that violates sanctions or export laws
- Activities that damage or interfere with our infrastructure

---

## 3. Network Abuse & Infrastructure Exploitation

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

- Distributed Denial of Service (**DDoS**) attacks
- Attempted intrusion into any network or system
- Port scanning or vulnerability scanning without authorization
- Brute-force attacks
- Exploiting software vulnerabilities
- Unauthorized access to data or accounts
- Running open proxy servers or anonymizing gateways
- Cryptocurrency mining on shared hosting
- Excessive resource usage impacting other customers

For **VPS and Cloud Server users**, security of your instance is your responsibility. However, abuse originating from your server will result in **immediate action**.

---

## 4. Spam & Email Abuse

You may **NOT** use our services to:

- Send unsolicited bulk email (**spam**)
- Operate mass mailing lists without opt-in consent
- Send phishing emails
- Send malware attachments
- Manipulate email headers

We enforce **strict anti-spam monitoring**. Accounts violating email policies may be **suspended without notice**.

---

## 5. Malware & Harmful Content

Prohibited content includes:

- Malware
- Ransomware
- Trojans
- Botnet command & control systems
- Exploit kits
- Keyloggers
- Remote access trojans
- Malicious scripts

We reserve the right to **scan and remove malicious content** to protect infrastructure stability.

---

## 6. Intellectual Property Violations

You may not host:

- Copyrighted material without authorization
- Pirated software
- Cracked applications
- Illegal streaming services
- Torrents distributing copyrighted works

Violations may result in **DMCA takedown and suspension**.

---

## 7. Adult & Sensitive Content

Adult content policies may vary depending on **jurisdiction and data center regulations**.

The following is strictly prohibited:

- Child exploitation content
- Non-consensual explicit material
- Human trafficking related material

Such content will be **reported to authorities**.

---

## 8. Resource Usage & Fair Use

Although NodeMania offers scalable infrastructure:

- Shared hosting accounts must **not excessively consume CPU, RAM, or I/O**
- Scripts causing instability may be **restricted**
- Automated bots must **not overload systems**
- Long-running background daemons are **not allowed on shared hosting**

**Cloud VPS customers** are allocated dedicated resources but must not impact **network integrity**.

---

## 9. Security Responsibility

Customers are responsible for:

- Securing their applications
- Updating CMS/plugins
- Using strong passwords
- Preventing compromise
- Monitoring their servers

Compromised accounts may be **temporarily suspended** to protect the network.

---

## 10. Monitoring & Enforcement

NodeMania reserves the right to:

- Monitor traffic patterns
- Investigate abuse reports
- Suspend or terminate accounts
- Remove malicious files
- Restrict network access
- Report criminal activity

We may act **without prior notice** in cases of severe abuse.
