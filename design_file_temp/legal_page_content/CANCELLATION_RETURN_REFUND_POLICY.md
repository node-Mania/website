# CANCELLATION / RETURN / REFUND POLICY

**Company:** NodeMania  
**Website:** https://www.nodemania.com  

---

## 1. Service Cancellation

Clients may cancel services through the **client portal** prior to the renewal date.

- Cancellation requests must be submitted **before the billing cycle renewal**.
- Services cancelled after the renewal date may be **charged for the next billing period**.

---

## 2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds will be processed **within 7–14 business days** and will be issued as **credit balance in your account**, unless otherwise stated.

Refunds may apply under the following conditions:

- New hosting accounts cancelled within **[7 / 14 / 30] days** (based on the selected refund window)
- **Duplicate payments**
- **Billing errors**

Refunds are **NOT provided** for:

- Domain registrations
- SSL certificates
- VPS services already provisioned
- Custom server configurations
- Add-ons and third-party licenses
- Accounts suspended for **policy violations**

---

## 3. VPS & Cloud Servers

Due to **instant provisioning and infrastructure allocation**, **VPS and Cloud Servers are non-refundable once deployed**.

---

## 4. Domain Names

Domain **registrations and renewals are final and non-refundable** due to **registry policies**.

---

## 5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed **within 7–14 business days** via the **original payment method** or **account credit**, depending on the payment provider and billing circumstances.

---

## 6. Chargebacks

Filing an **unjustified chargeback** may result in:

- **Permanent account termination**
- **Suspension of all services**
- **Recovery action for outstanding balances**
